How this number was reached
  1. Your inputs. A problem that affects 10% of users; you want an 80% chance of seeing it at least once.
  2. One participant misses it 90% of the time, so all n participants miss it with probability 0.90n.
  3. Seeing it at least once = 1 − 0.90n. Set that to at least 0.80.
  4. Solve for n. n = ln(0.20) ÷ ln(0.90) = 15.3, rounded up to 16.
