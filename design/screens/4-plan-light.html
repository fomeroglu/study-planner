How these numbers were reached

★ Task time
  1. Your inputs. Smallest difference that matters: 10 seconds. At that difference, 8 of 10 participants would be faster (your estimate).
  2. Variability. Individual differences vary by about 11.9 seconds. Your estimate, not measured.
  3. Effect size. 10 ÷ 11.9 ≈ 0.84 (Cohen's dz).
  4. Alpha. 0.05 split across 2 comparisons = 0.025 each.
  5. Sample size. Paired t-test at power 0.80 gives 17. Adding 5% for Wilcoxon signed-rank gives 18.
  6. Order plan. 18 fits 6 orders exactly: 3 participants each.
